## Introduction

MyWorkoutLog ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application MyWorkoutLog (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Personal Data
- **Workout Data**: Exercise routines, sets, reps, weights, duration, rest times, and notes
- **Health Metrics**: Body weight, personal records, and performance analytics
- **Media Content**: Video references attached to workout sets (stored locally or in your cloud storage)
- **Usage Data**: App interaction patterns, feature usage, and performance metrics

### Automatically Collected Data
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Technical Data**: App version, crash reports, error logs, and performance metrics
- **Analytics Data**: Screen views, button clicks, and user flow patterns (anonymized)

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Workout Tracking**: Store and display your exercise data and progress
- **Performance Analytics**: Generate insights, charts, and personal records
- **App Functionality**: Provide core features like timers, templates, and program management
- **Data Backup**: Enable cloud backup and synchronization across devices

### Secondary Uses
- **App Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
- **Technical Support**: Troubleshoot issues and provide customer support
- **Security**: Detect and prevent unauthorized access or fraudulent activity

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All workout data is primarily stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite database
- Local data is protected by Android's application sandbox and device security features
- No sensitive data is transmitted without your explicit consent

### Cloud Backup (Optional)
- Cloud backup is entirely optional and requires your explicit authorization
- When enabled, encrypted workout data is stored in your personal Google Drive
- We do not have access to your Google Drive or the ability to decrypt your backed-up data
- You maintain full control over your cloud-stored data

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: All sensitive data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption
- **Access Control**: Strict access controls and authentication mechanisms
- **Regular Updates**: Security patches and updates are applied regularly
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not use third-party analytics services that could access your data

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do Not Share Your Data
- **No Sales**: We never sell your personal data to third parties
- **No Marketing**: We do not share data with advertisers or marketing companies
- **No Analytics Services**: We do not use third-party analytics that access your personal data

### Limited Exceptions
We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by law, regulation, or legal process
- **Safety Protection**: To protect the safety of users or the public
- **Business Transfer**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notification)

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Access**: View all data stored by the app through the built-in interface
- **Export**: Export your complete workout history in standard formats
- **Delete**: Permanently delete your data from the app and local storage
- **Portability**: Transfer your data to other fitness tracking applications

### Cloud Backup Control
- **Enable/Disable**: Turn cloud backup on or off at any time
- **Delete Cloud Data**: Remove backed-up data from your Google Drive
- **Selective Backup**: Choose which data types to include in backups

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)
If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:
- **Right to Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification**: Correct inaccurate personal data
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Right to Portability**: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Object**: Object to data processing based on legitimate interests

## Data Retention

### Local Data
- Data remains on your device until you delete the app or manually delete the data
- You have complete control over data retention periods

### Cloud Backup Data  
- Backed-up data remains in your Google Drive according to your Google account settings
- We recommend regularly reviewing and managing your cloud-stored data

### Automatic Deletion
- Crash logs and error reports are automatically deleted after 90 days
- Temporary files and cache data are cleared regularly by the system

## Children's Privacy

MyWorkoutLog is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

## International Data Transfers

As we primarily use local storage and your personal cloud storage, international data transfers are minimal. When they do occur:
- Data is encrypted during transmission
- We comply with applicable data protection laws
- Adequate safeguards are in place to protect your data
